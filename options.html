Live Translate

Real-time translation of the audio playing in your browser tab

1 Enter your API key and target language below, then click Save
2 Open the tab you want translated (YouTube, a webinar, Zoom Web…)
3 Click the extension icon — hear the translation and read the subtitles. Click again to stop

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (no payment method required). The Live Translate model is included in the free tier, so it costs nothing — exactly as when you use aistudio.google.com/live directly — as long as you stay within the free quota. Your key is stored only in this browser on your own machine, and is sent only to Google's servers.

If the translated speech keeps breaking up mid-sentence, choose Favor smoothness. The buffer is adaptive: it grows when the network becomes unstable and shrinks back down once the connection is steady, so the delay always stays as low as your network allows.

Optional, off by default. Removes short fillers such as “um”, “uh”, “ööö” and “izé”, plus clearly comma-separated phrases like “you know,” and “szóval,”. This changes on-screen subtitles only; it cannot edit the voice generated by Google's live translation model. Some legitimate discourse markers may be omitted.

Optional, off by default. Restores the original tab audio at normal volume whenever the translated voice is silent and smoothly lowers it while translated voice plays. The extension does not separate music from original-language speech.

Tip: keep the source tab's audio clear and use a stable connection for the smoothest translation. This extension collects no data and sends nothing anywhere except to Google's servers.
